RIFTWARD

A PEACEFUL FANTASY WORLD TO WANDER, SCAN AND LEARN

Long ago, a gentle Rift opened above a green kingdom and scattered its light into 7 Ancient Runestones hidden across forests, meadows and old ruins. Nothing here is dangerous — the valley is waiting to be explored, not conquered.

You are a young Riftwarden — an explorer and researcher with a lantern, a satchel and a field journal. Wander the open world, follow the gold arrow to every Runestone, and stop along the way to scan glowing discoveries: mushrooms that make their own light, crystals that sing, fossils, sundials, bees and moths. Each scan teaches you something real and fills a page of your journal. Gather all 7 Runestones and bring them home to the World Shrine to restore the Rift and light the valley again.

🌲 Explore an Open WorldForest, village, meadows, stone ruins and lantern-lit paths around a central shrine. Go anywhere — no rails, no loading screens.
🔍 Scan & LearnFind 8 glowing discovery spots — plants, crystals, ruins and gentle creatures. Tap SCAN nearby to observe each one and learn a real science or nature fact.
💎 How You WinCollect 7 / 7 Runestones, then return to the World Shrine and tap SCAN / TALK to restore the Rift. The gold arrow always points to your next stone.
📖 Fill Your JournalEvery discovery and Runestone earns XP and score. Level up as an explorer and open your JOURNAL any time to read what you have found.
🏘️ Talk & DiscoverVillagers are researchers and guides. Walk up and tap SCAN / TALK for hints about the valley and where to explore next.
📱 Built for Your PhoneBig friendly touch controls: joystick left, SCAN / RUN / JUMP right. Also works with WASD, Space, Shift and E on desktop.

LEFT STICK — Walk  •  SCAN / TALK — Observe a discovery, chat with a villager, or restore the Rift at the shrine  •  RUN — Quick burst (uses energy, which refills as you walk)  •  JUMP — Hop over rocks and roots  •  Follow the gold arrow at the top — the distance counts down in metres. Glowing spots that are not Runestones are discoveries: stand close and tap SCAN to learn about them.

Riftward is a peaceful exploration game: wander → discover → learn → collect 7 Runestones → restore the Rift → win.
